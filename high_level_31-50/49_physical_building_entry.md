# 49 — Break Into a Building in an Authorized / Legal Manner

## What to Do
With written authorization from the building owner or security manager, attempt physical penetration: tailgating, social engineering the front desk, bypassing badge readers, or testing whether "I'm from IT" gets you past security — then report what worked.

## Open Source Resources
- "The Art of Intrusion" by Kevin Mitnick (for technique context)
- DEF CON Social Engineering Village talks (YouTube)
- PTES physical security testing guidelines

## Tips
- Written authorization is non-negotiable — verbal is not enough. Carry it on your person during the test.
- Bring a "get out of jail free" letter from the authorizing manager with a contact number.
- Start with observation before attempting entry — watch how legitimate people enter and what the guards actually check.

## When It's Completed
You've produced a written report of what access was gained, what controls failed, what was caught, and specific remediation recommendations for each failed control.
