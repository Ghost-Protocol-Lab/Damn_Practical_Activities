# 03 — Complete the Week's Trace Labs Challenges

## What to Do
Complete the current week's OSINT challenges on the Trace Labs platform (missing-persons-style practice scenarios) using only legal, ethical OSINT methods.

## Open Source Resources
- tracelabs.org
- Trace Labs OSINT VM (pre-loaded toolset)
- Trace Labs Discord community for technique discussion

## Tips
- Document your methodology as you go, not just the final answers — process matters more than the result.
- Use the official OSINT VM to keep your toolset consistent with the community standard.
- Cross-check findings against at least two independent sources before logging them.

## When It's Completed
All challenges for the week are submitted, and you have a written methodology log detailed enough that someone else could replicate your findings from it.
