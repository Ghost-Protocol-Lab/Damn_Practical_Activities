# 04 — Send a Phishing Email to a Friend (With Permission)

## What to Do
With explicit written consent, craft and send a realistic but harmless phishing email to a friend to test their awareness, then debrief regardless of the outcome.

## Open Source Resources
- GoPhish (open-source phishing simulation framework) — getgophish.com
- Google's Phishing Quiz (for studying real examples)
- SANS phishing awareness templates (for study, not reuse verbatim)

## Tips
- Never include real credential harvesting or malicious payloads — the goal is awareness, not actual compromise.
- Use a sandboxed/fake landing page if you want to track click-through.
- Get consent in writing, even informally (a text message confirming agreement is enough).

## When It's Completed
The email is sent, you've recorded whether it was clicked or reported, and you've had an open debrief explaining the red flags they missed or caught.
