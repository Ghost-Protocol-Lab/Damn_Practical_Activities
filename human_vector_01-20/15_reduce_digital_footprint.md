# 15 — Reduce Your Digital Footprint in a Day

## What to Do
Spend a day actively minimizing your exposed personal data — deleting unused accounts, opting out of data brokers, and tightening privacy settings on accounts you keep.

## Open Source Resources
- JustDeleteMe (database of account deletion links)
- Privacy Rights Clearinghouse data broker opt-out list
- DeleteMe (paid alternative, if you want it automated)
- Your password manager's saved-login list (to find forgotten accounts)

## Tips
- Start with data brokers — biggest exposure, easiest wins.
- Then close old, forgotten accounts surfaced by your password manager.
- Tighten privacy settings on the accounts you're keeping last.

## When It's Completed
You've documented every account closed or opted out, and compared your Have I Been Pwned / data broker search results from before and after.
