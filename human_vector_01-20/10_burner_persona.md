# 10 — Form a Complete Burner OSINT Persona

## What to Do
Build a complete, internally consistent fictional identity across multiple platforms (email, one or two social accounts, a believable backstory) purely as investigative tradecraft practice — not to deceive real individuals for personal gain.

## Open Source Resources
- IntelTechniques' persona-building guide
- Fake Name Generator
- ProtonMail or Tutanota (for a burner email)
- A password manager, to keep persona credentials fully separate from your real identity

## Tips
- Consistency is everything — mismatched join dates, writing style, or timezone activity are what blow a persona.
- Keep a "persona bible" document tracking every invented detail so you don't contradict yourself later.
- Never link the persona to real payment methods or your real phone number.

## When It's Completed
The persona is active and consistent across at least two platforms for a full week, and you can't find an inconsistency when you audit it yourself.
