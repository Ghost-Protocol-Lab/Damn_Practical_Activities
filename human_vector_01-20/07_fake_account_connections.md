# 07 — Make a Fake Social Media Account and Map Its Connections

## What to Do
Build a believable but fictional "sock puppet" persona and observe who it attracts, connects with, or interacts with over time. This is standard investigator tradecraft, not a tool for targeting real people.

## Open Source Resources
- IntelTechniques' sock puppet creation guide
- Fake Name Generator (for consistent fictional details)
- AI-generated non-identifiable profile images (used carefully, and never to impersonate a real person)

## Tips
- Never impersonate a real, identifiable person.
- Keep the persona low-key at first — an overly interesting persona attracts scrutiny quickly.
- Log every connection and interaction with a timestamp.

## When It's Completed
You've documented the persona's growth (connections, interactions) over at least a week and reflected on which behaviors made it seem credible or raised suspicion.
