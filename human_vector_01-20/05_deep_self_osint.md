# 05 — Very Detailed OSINT on Self or a Friend (With Permission)

## What to Do
Go beyond surface-level OSINT: chase data broker listings, old forum accounts, breach data, cached/archived pages, and cross-reference usernames across years of activity.

## Open Source Resources
- Have I Been Pwned — haveibeenpwned.com
- Wayback Machine — archive.org
- IntelTechniques tools (Michael Bazzell's OSINT resources)
- Epieos (email-to-account lookup)
- Privacy Rights Clearinghouse data broker list

## Tips
- Agree on boundaries in advance (e.g., no financial records) — this type of search can get uncomfortably thorough fast.
- Keep a running spreadsheet of every source found, so takedown requests are easy later.
- Check for old, forgotten accounts tied to old email addresses or usernames.

## When It's Completed
You've produced a full report of every data source found, categorized by sensitivity, plus a remediation checklist of what to remove and where to opt out.
