# 09 — Play GeoGuessr and Achieve a 3-Win Streak

## What to Do
Play GeoGuessr in multiplayer/duel mode against a real opponent and win three rounds in a row using visual clues only.

## Open Source Resources
- geoguessr.com
- GeoGuessr technique breakdowns (e.g., Rainbolt's YouTube channel)
- A personal "clue cheat sheet" — driving side of road, bollard types, pole colors by country

## Tips
- Learn the small set of high-value "meta" clues first: Street View car/camera generation, sun position, road markings, language on signage.
- Speed comes from repetition more than raw geography knowledge — play daily.
- Narrow by continent first, then country, then region before committing to a pin.

## When It's Completed
You've achieved a verified 3-round win streak against a live opponent (a screenshot of the result is enough proof).
