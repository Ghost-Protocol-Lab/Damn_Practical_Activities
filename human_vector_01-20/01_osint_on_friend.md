# 01 — OSINT on a Friend (With Permission)

## What to Do
Conduct a structured open-source intelligence investigation on a consenting friend. Map their public digital footprint: social media profiles, public posts, metadata, employer info, and usernames reused across platforms. Treat it like a real engagement — define scope before you start.

## Open Source Resources
- OSINT Framework — osintframework.com
- Sherlock (username search across platforms) — github.com/sherlock-project/sherlock
- theHarvester (email/subdomain gathering)
- Maltego CE (link analysis, free tier)
- Google dorking cheat sheets

## Tips
- Get explicit consent and agree on scope (which platforms, how deep) before starting.
- Start with username pivoting, then move to employer/public records.
- Keep a timestamped log of every source you check.

## When It's Completed
You've produced a written report mapping their public footprint, found at least one thing they didn't know was public, and walked them through the findings afterward, including how to reduce exposure.
