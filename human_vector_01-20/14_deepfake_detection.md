# 14 — Deepfake / Voice-Clone Detection Test

## What to Do
Practice distinguishing real media from AI-generated audio/video using a mix of known-real and known-fake samples.

## Open Source Resources
- MIT's "Detect Fakes" project
- Deepfake Detection Challenge dataset (public samples)
- Voice-clone demo tools (used ethically on your own voice only, for comparison samples)
- Jigsaw's "Assembler" research writeups

## Tips
- Look for unnatural blinking patterns, lighting inconsistencies, and lip-sync drift in video.
- For audio, listen for overly smooth prosody, inconsistent breathing, or a lack of natural background noise.
- Score yourself blind first, then review which cue actually gave it away each time.

## When It's Completed
You've scored yourself against a labeled set of 10+ samples and can articulate which specific cue tipped you off in each case.
