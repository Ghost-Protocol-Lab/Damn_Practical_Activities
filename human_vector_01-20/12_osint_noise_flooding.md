# 12 — Make One OSINT Tactic Ineffective Through Noise

## What to Do
Pick one specific OSINT technique (e.g., username correlation or reverse image search) and deliberately generate enough plausible-but-false data about yourself to degrade that technique's reliability when used against you. This is a defensive privacy exercise.

## Open Source Resources
- Username generators (for creating decoy accounts)
- EXIF editing tools (for altering metadata on decoy images you post yourself)
- Guides on "privacy through obfuscation" (distinct from anonymity) — IntelTechniques covers this

## Tips
- Keep decoys clearly separated from your real accounts in your own private records, so you don't confuse yourself later.
- Focus on one technique at a time so you can measure the effect clearly.
- This is about raising noise, not spreading false information that could mislead or harm others — keep decoys harmless and clearly about you only.

## When It's Completed
Re-running the chosen OSINT technique on yourself shows a measurable drop in confidence or accuracy compared to before.
