# 16 — Find a New Circle of Friends and Build Rapport

## What to Do
Meet a new group of people and practice genuine rapport-building — active listening, remembering details, asking good follow-up questions. These are the same human-trust skills that underlie both social-engineering defense and everyday relationships.

## Open Source Resources
- "Never Split the Difference" by Chris Voss (structured conversation and listening technique)
- Local meetups, clubs, or classes as a venue to meet people
- Articles on active listening technique (mirroring, labeling, open questions)

## Tips
- Treat this as building real relationships, not as practicing manipulation on unwitting subjects.
- The value here is understanding what builds genuine trust, so you can better recognize when those same cues are used against you.
- Follow up with people afterward — rapport that doesn't persist isn't rapport, it's a transaction.

## When It's Completed
You've had meaningful conversations with at least 3–5 new people and can reflect on which listening and questioning techniques actually built rapport versus felt forced.
