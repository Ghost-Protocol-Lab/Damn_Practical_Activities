# 02 — GeoOSINT on an Image

## What to Do
Given a photo with no metadata, identify its exact or approximate location using only visual cues: vegetation, signage, architecture, shadows, language on signs, license plates, road markings.

## Open Source Resources
- GeoGuessr — geoguessr.com (for practice reps)
- Bellingcat's Online Investigation Toolkit
- SunCalc.org (shadow-based time/direction estimation)
- Google Earth / Street View
- r/whereisthis and similar communities

## Tips
- Work top-down: language/script narrows the country, architecture narrows the region, specific landmarks narrow the spot.
- Shadows + SunCalc can estimate time of day and rough latitude.
- Note vehicle types, plate colors, and pole/bollard styles — these vary distinctly by country.

## When It's Completed
You've correctly identified the location (verified via Street View or satellite match) or narrowed it to a specific city/region with documented reasoning for every clue you used.
